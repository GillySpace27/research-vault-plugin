# AI provenance

Who wrote what, and who verified it. This is the disclosure and audit trail for
AI involvement in this manuscript. It answers a different question from
[`05_ledger/provenance.md`](05_ledger/provenance.md), which records which script
produced which number; conflating the two loses both.

Gilly is the sole author (D21) and takes responsibility for every claim here.
Claude (Anthropic) was used at every stage. The point of this file is that the
disclosure is specific enough to audit rather than a blanket sentence.

## Roles

This notation is **project-local**, not a community standard and not a journal's
scheme. It exists because no standard disclosure format records per-item origin
and verification. Define it in the paper if you cite it.

Two independent axes. **Origin** answers "where did this come from", and
**verification** answers "who or what checked it". Every row needs one of each,
and the second is the one that matters.

**Origin**

| Code | Meaning | Example in this repo |
|---|---|---|
| `H` | Human-originated. The idea or decision is Gilly's. | <the core idea> | `H` | | Say plainly whether the central idea was AI-originated. |

## What AI was not used for

- The invention of the method.
- The decision of what to claim, or the scientific judgement behind D20–D26.
- Any number reported as measured. Every such number traces to a script in
  `tools/` or a cited source, both of which are re-runnable by a referee.
